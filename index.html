03 / A more useful shortlist

A Litbuy shopping guide that starts with the details.

When researching Litbuy finds, a short list with clear evidence is easier to use than a long list of unexplained links.

Keep your chosen item, variant and comparison notes together. Recheck the original listing before any purchase.

  1. Choose one category and one purpose.

    Decide whether you need daily sneakers, a layering hoodie, a jersey or a specific accessory. Set a budget and preferred fit before comparing listings.

  2. Capture the exact listing.

    Save the source URL, item name, selected color, size, displayed currency and the date checked. Similar photos can appear on different listings.

  3. Compare measurements and evidence.

    Ask for measurements where needed. Review relevant item photos and distinguish customer images from promotional photography.

  4. Confirm the full ordering terms.

    Check availability, seller identity, delivery options and return conditions. Treat spreadsheet prices and shipping estimates as provisional until confirmed.

06 / Clear answers

Before you follow a product link.

Practical answers for readers comparing spreadsheet finds and browsing CAFANS.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In shopping searches, a Litbuy spreadsheet usually means a curated product-link list used to organize finds. A useful list records categories, source links, sizes, prices and review notes. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify a product or seller.

Does this page provide an official Litbuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent editorial guide and CAFANS category directory. It does not host an official Litbuy spreadsheet, provide Litbuy checkout or claim a partnership with Litbuy.

How should I compare sneaker and clothing finds?

For sneakers, compare the exact model, size system, insole measurements and construction photos. For clothing, compare garment measurements, fabric descriptions, fit and return terms. Match the details to the exact item and variant.

Is the listed product price the final cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the seller and ordering route, the final cost can include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the complete quote before paying.

Do quality-control photos prove authenticity?

No. Photos can help reveal visible condition, dimensions and differences, but they do not independently prove authenticity. For branded goods, check seller provenance, purchase documentation and any available professional authentication.

Where do the category links on this page go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS categories. General browse buttons open www.cafans.com. These destinations are separate from Litbuy.
